Hi! I’m Jay, and I am a 2D and 3D artist from Portland, Oregon. I am a soon-to-be Interactive Media and Game Development - Technical Art/Computer Science graduate from WPI. I have a background in both digital and traditional art – particularly in character design. Throughout my time at WPI I havbe been involved in various projects and aspects of the game-making process. In IMGD 3500: Artistic Game Development I, I created a walking simulator in 7 weeks from the ground up, and in IMGD 4500: Artistic Game Development II, I worked with a team of other students to create a roguelite game for IMGD's Showfest. I modelled & rigged our player character, created particle effects with Unreal's Niagara system, and modelled & textured player & enemy abilities. Both of these games can be found linked on my Itch.io. My most recent project is my Major Qualifying Project, Disillusion. I have been at the head of environment & character design, and will be continuing to work on character modelling, rigging, as well as coding the game's puzzle stystems. Disillusion is projected to have a minimum viable product by May 2026.
